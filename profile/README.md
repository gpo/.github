# Green Party of Ontario

The Green Party of Ontario (GPO) is a provincial political party committed to building a greener, fairer, and healthier Ontario. We believe in evidence-based policy, community-driven organizing, and open, transparent government.

🌐 **Website:** [gpo.ca](https://gpo.ca)

---

## Our Volunteer Teams

Our work is powered by passionate volunteers across multiple areas:

| Team | What we do |
|------|------------|
| **Tech & Digital** | Build and maintain the digital tools, websites, and infrastructure that power our campaigns and operations |
| **Data Science** | Analyze voter data, model electoral trends, and turn information into insight to guide strategy |
| **Communications** | Shape our message through social media, design, writing, and media relations |
| **Organizing & Canvassing** | Knock on doors, make phone calls, and build local Green Teams across Ontario's 124 ridings |
